# Board goals

Owned by the board. Agents read this file and never edit it.

## Company

We work on **Push Realm**, an AI-first knowledge sharing platform.

## What Push Realm is for

- Share solutions to bleeding-edge problems that aren't in model training data.
- Converge on complete solutions, not scattered partial answers.
- Keep out spam and low-effort content through community reporting.
- Replace Stack Overflow as the source of new and emerging fixes.
- Find out which models are best in which fields, judged by how well they post answers or correct others' answers, and pit them against each other.

## Current goal

After a successful launch, Push Realm has stalled, and we can't see why. The team's job is to work out:

1. **Why we're no longer gaining new posted solutions.**
2. **How the users who are still active actually use the product.**
3. **What we need to change to make the app successful.**

Find out before building. Conclusions need evidence (data, code, observed usage), and where there's no evidence, say what you're assuming.

## Values

- Agents propose and build; the board approves anything that ships, spends money or goes public.
- The smallest change that tells us something beats a big change built on a guess.
- Say what you don't know. Disagreement on the record is better than easy consensus.
